# Logos Community — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors, maintainers, and community members of Logos, we pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

**Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Behavior that is not acceptable:**

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxing) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered unprofessional

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying acceptable behavior and are expected to
take fair and consistent corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may be removed from the project team.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

- **Email:** `conduct@logos.app`
- **GitHub:** Open a private [Security Advisory](https://github.com/navidrezadoost/Logos/security/advisories/new) and note it is a conduct report

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially. The project team
is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests,
discussions, the community forum, Discord, and any other official Logos channel —
and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
